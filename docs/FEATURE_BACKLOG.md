# Backlog Haven - Feature Backlog

This backlog tracks deferred feature ideas, UI experiments, and improvements that may be revisited later. Entries are exploratory unless their status changes; inclusion here does not approve them for implementation.

## Status

- **Idea:** Captured for possible future consideration.
- **Exploring:** Being researched or prototyped; not approved.
- **Planned:** Approved for future implementation.
- **Implemented:** Completed in the application.

## Data terminology

- **Platform:** The platform associated with the game or release, such as PS5, PC, or Nintendo Switch.
- **Device:** The physical system used to play the game, such as a PlayStation 5 or ROG Ally.

These concepts must remain distinct even when a UI experiment displays one or both on game artwork.
